Background

A caseload that runs on precision, not just volume.

Priya has spent the last five years at the intersection of immigration law and HR operations inside a high-volume IT staffing environment — where a single missed deadline or incomplete filing can cost a candidate their status. That pressure shaped how she works: methodical documentation, proactive client communication, and a caseload that never falls behind.

Her immigration work spans the full employment-based lifecycle — H-1B petitions (new, extension, and transfer), and PERM/Green Card processing from Prevailing Wage Determination through Labor Certification — all handled in close coordination with supervising attorneys. Alongside that, she's run the HR side of the business: onboarding, I-9/E-Verify compliance, benefits, employee relations, and vendor contracts, giving her a rare full-picture view of what a case means for the person behind it.
